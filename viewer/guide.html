    Model sources & service notes

    This Brax3 visual service draft uses artwork and the reference render supplied in the Brax3 KeyShot package. The interactive model combines the original E115 CAD chassis with reconstructed rear glass, carrier, NFC assembly, battery and fasteners. The native KeyShot scene has not been exported into this viewer. No photograph of assembled internals is painted onto the cover.

    The package is a visualization source, not a service manual. Screw specifications, adhesive-release procedure, connector positions and NFC contact arrangement need verification against the actual Brax3. No screw counts, adhesive-tab order or lighting-hardware steps from another handset are assumed.

    Tool photographs: Benjamin Hockings and contributors / iFixit, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Brax3 instructions and interactive illustrations have been rewritten for this draft.
